# 📦 GymSwipe!!
HEY! I'm Tom a software engineer intern at national grid and an avid fitness enthusiast, I'm building GymSwipe to 
combine these two passions of mine!

## 🌟 Highlights
- Encourage yourself to get out their and hit your fitness goals. 
- Tinder like functionality to either accept or reject your daily workout. 
- Gamification is added too, grow your pokemon with your own healthy choices. 


## ℹ️ Overview
Being a software engineer and a full time athlete I can see first hand how difficult it can be to balance your health, career and all other commitments, but fact is everything is a concious decision its a match for your or a rejection. Being upfront with it will often help tackle it head one and thats what GymSwipe aims to do. 

- Tinder for you and your workouts! Swipe and face your discipline head on. 
- Swipe right to show you completed a workout & left in case you didn't, a pokemon icon will gain or lose exp from that
- This tackles a common issue humans have, we have to face our daily choices and be aware of if we are facing them or not
